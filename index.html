About Beachside Circle Boats

Experience a new way to explore Orange Beach

Beachside Circle Boats brings a whole new way to experience Orange Beach. Our portable, eco-friendly electric circle boats are perfect for cruising the intercoastal waterways — no gas, no noise, just pure fun on the water.

Perfect For: Families, couples, birthday parties, group outings, fishing, or just relaxing on the water. No boating license needed!
