Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Beachside Circle Boats ("we," "us," or "our") operates through the CyberCheck platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our booking services or receive SMS communications from us.

Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number provided during booking
  • Booking details including date, time, boat type, and payment information
  • SMS opt-in consent status
  • Device and usage data when you visit our website

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your boat rental booking
  • To send SMS booking confirmations and reminders (only with your consent)
  • To process payments securely via Stripe
  • To send post-trip review requests
  • To improve our services

SMS Communications

By checking the SMS opt-in box during booking, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages including booking confirmations, reminders, and updates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your bookings.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. To get help, reply HELP or contact us at (601) 325-1205.

We do not share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to operate our services (payment processing via Stripe, SMS delivery via Twilio). We do not share your information for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your personal information. Payment data is processed securely by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at beachsideboats@myyahoo.com.
